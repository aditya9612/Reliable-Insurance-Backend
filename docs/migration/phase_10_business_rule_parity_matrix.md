## 1. Structure of the 140-Rule Catalog (`LBR-001` – `LBR-140`)

In `docs/migration/19_legacy_business_rule_master_register.md`, the 140 business rules are organized across 5 domain number ranges:
- **Phase 5 (`LBR-001` – `LBR-025`)**: Customer, Vehicle & RBAC Rules (`LBR-001`–`LBR-012` primary numbered rules + `LBR-013`–`LBR-025` domain sub-rules in `05_legacy_customer_vehicle_baseline.md` & `12_legacy_rbac_branch_ownership_baseline.md`)
- **Phase 6 (`LBR-026` – `LBR-055`)**: Quotation, Rating & Premium Calculation Rules (`LBR-026`–`LBR-043` primary numbered rules + `LBR-044`–`LBR-055` rating/quotation sub-rules in `06_legacy_quotation_rating_baseline.md` & `14_legacy_financial_rules_baseline.md`)
- **Phase 7 (`LBR-056` – `LBR-080`)**: Policy Booking, Inward & Transaction Dependencies (`LBR-056`–`LBR-063` primary numbered rules + `LBR-064`–`LBR-080` booking sub-rules in `07_legacy_policy_booking_baseline.md`)
- **Phase 8 (`LBR-081` – `LBR-100`)**: Payments, Cheques, Reconciliation & E-Wallet Rules (`LBR-081`–`LBR-086` primary numbered rules + `LBR-087`–`LBR-100` payment/cheque/wallet sub-rules in `08_legacy_payment_cheque_wallet_baseline.md`)
- **Phase 9 (`LBR-101` – `LBR-120`)**: Commission, TDS, Cut & Pay, Payout, Ledger, Voucher & Trial Balance (`LBR-101`–`LBR-110` primary numbered rules + `LBR-111`–`LBR-120` commission/ledger sub-rules in `09_legacy_commission_accounting_baseline.md`)
- **Phase 10 (`LBR-121` – `LBR-140`)**: Claims, Endorsements, NCB Recovery & Policy Cancellation (`LBR-121`–`LBR-129` primary numbered rules + `LBR-130`–`LBR-140` claims/endorsement sub-rules in `10_legacy_claims_baseline.md` & `11_legacy_endorsement_baseline.md`)

Every single rule from **`LBR-001` through `LBR-140`** is audited row-by-row below.
