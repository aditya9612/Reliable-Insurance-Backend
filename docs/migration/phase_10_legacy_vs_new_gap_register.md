## 1. Classification & Severity Legend

### Allowed Parity Classifications
1. **`PARITY`** — Exact business behavior match between legacy C#/.NET baseline and FastAPI implementation.
2. **`INTENTIONAL HARDENING`** — Security, validation, concurrency (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`), atomic transaction boundary, or idempotency improvement that does not alter valid business or financial outcomes.
3. **`LEGACY DEFECT MITIGATED`** — Known legacy bug (`DEF-001` through `DEF-011`) intentionally corrected with full documentation and regression coverage.
4. **`DEVIATION`** — New implementation differs materially from legacy business or financial behavior without an documented legacy defect justification.
5. **`MISSING`** — Legacy capability documented in the baseline is not implemented in the new backend (either omitted in Phases 5–10 or deferred to Phases 11–15).
6. **`UNKNOWN — EVIDENCE NOT AVAILABLE`** — Legacy behavior resides inside unextracted MySQL stored procedure bodies, live DB triggers, or production master tables not present in the C# source baseline.

### Risk / Severity Levels
- **`P0` (Critical)** — Material financial, ledger/accounting, or core business workflow deviation affecting monetary records or general ledger integrity.
- **`P1` (High)** — Significant business rule, tax rate, endorsement workflow, or accounting code mismatch affecting subsets of policies or transactions.
- **`P2` (Medium)** — Secondary workflow omission, insurer-specific edge case, sub-entity simplification, or rounding precision difference (`±1 INR`).
- **`P3` (Low)** — Deferred non-core utility, reporting/file/OCR/notification feature scheduled for Phases 11–15, or cosmetic/schema-naming difference with alias compatibility.
